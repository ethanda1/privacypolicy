# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

## Overview

CoTake ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Camera & Photos
- **Camera Access**: When you take photos within the app, we request permission to access your device's camera. Photos are only captured when you explicitly choose to take them and send them to friends.
- **Storage**: Photos you take are stored on our servers (Supabase) and associated with your account. You control who can see your photos through privacy settings.

### Account Information
- **Email & Authentication**: To create an account, we collect your email address. We use Supabase's authentication service to securely manage your login credentials.
- **Profile Data**: Username, display name, avatar, and other profile information you choose to provide.

### Usage Data
- **App Activity**: We collect information about which features you use and when, to improve the app and fix bugs.
- **Device Info**: Basic device type and OS version for technical support.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the App's core functionality (sending takes, messaging, photos)
- To authenticate your account
- To improve app performance and fix issues
- To send you notifications you've opted into
- To enforce our terms of service

## Data Storage & Security

- All data (photos, messages, account info) is stored on Supabase servers
- We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit
- Your photos are only visible to people you choose to share with (friends, or friends-of-friends based on your privacy settings)

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Supabase**: Backend database and authentication (see [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy))
- **Expo**: App distribution and updates (see [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy))

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Access** your data by viewing your profile and takes in the app
- **Delete** your account and all associated data at any time through Settings → Delete Account
- **Change** your privacy settings to control who sees your posts

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Data Deletion

If you delete your account:
- Your profile is permanently removed
- All photos and posts are permanently deleted
- Your personal data is erased from our servers
- Friendships and any pending requests are cancelled

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete such information.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
